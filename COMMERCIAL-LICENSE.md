# Commercial & OEM Licensing

FieldRuntime is available under two tracks:

| Track | License | For whom |
|---|---|---|
| **Community** | MPL-2.0 (default) | Anyone. Free to use, modify, and integrate, including in closed-source products — provided modifications to MPL-covered files are published. |
| **Commercial / OEM** | Private commercial license | Vendors who need to modify FieldRuntime's MPL-covered files **without** publishing those modifications, or who want warranty, indemnity, priority support, or brand-use rights. |

## When you need a commercial license

Under MPL-2.0 you are already free to:

- Use unmodified FieldRuntime in a closed-source product.
- Add closed-source code **around** it (web UI, business plugins, product
  shell, cloud platform) as long as those are separate files.
- Link it statically or dynamically into a proprietary program.

You should consider a **commercial license** if you want to:

- Modify FieldRuntime's own `.h/.cpp` files and keep those changes private.
- Avoid the file-level source-disclosure obligation entirely.
- Receive a warranty, indemnification, or an SLA-backed support contract.
- Use the FieldRuntime name/logo to brand or endorse a product
  (see [`TRADEMARKS.md`](TRADEMARKS.md)).
- Arrange a corporate CLA for your team's contributions.

## What a commercial license typically includes

- A non-MPL grant to modify covered files without source disclosure.
- Optional priority support and maintenance.
- Optional warranty / indemnity terms.
- Optional trademark-use rights.

hardware/platform, and whether you intend to modify core files.
